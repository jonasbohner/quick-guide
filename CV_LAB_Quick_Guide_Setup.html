Before recording

Setup & Calibrate

How to Set Up the Camera
  1. 1
    Set up the tripod on the mid-court line and mount the camera. Plug it into a power bank or wall outlet.
  2. 2
    Run the KINEXON app and turn the camera screen to face the left half of the court, then follow the steps shown on the screen.
  3. 3
    Calibrate — line up the mid-court line between the two red markers on the screen.
  4. 4
    Start the timer — set a 1–30 min delay and tap "Go!"
  5. 5
    Raise the camera to its maximum height (~3 m / 12 ft). If you hear an audio signal, the camera's orientation is incorrect and needs correcting — check the screen colors below.

02
During the session

Recording & Stop

  • Keep the camera fully stationary — make sure it's safe and nobody touches it. Movement lowers video quality.
  • If the camera does get moved, put it back and make sure it faces the same way as at the start.
  • The session length shows live on the red screen.
  • To finish: loosen the screws of the tripod, lower the camera, then press Stop.
03
After the session

Upload

Important: keep the camera powered the whole time it's recording and uploading, or you could lose the footage.
Option 1
Direct Upload
Connected to Wi-Fi? Tap "Upload Now." The video uploads on its own once it finds a connection — even if you move the camera within the same network.
Option 2
On-Demand Upload
No Wi-Fi? The recording is saved under "Manage Videos." Once you're back on Wi-Fi or Ethernet, open Manage Videos, select the session, and tap Upload.
Before You Start
—Battery and power bank charged
—Camera is powered during upload
—Tripod on mid-court
—Camera safe and stationary
—Screen shows green and camera has no signal before recording
